About Sandaman Constructions

Built On Site Experience, Not Shortcuts.

Twelve years of grading roads, pouring foundations and finishing buildings across Ghana — under one standard of workmanship.

Our Story

From One Grader to a Full Fleet

Sandaman Constructions started as a small earthworks outfit and grew into a multi-disciplinary contractor by keeping every promise on every contract, however small.

Sandaman Constructions Ltd was founded to close a simple gap: too many infrastructure contracts in Ghana were won on price and lost on execution. We built our name on the opposite approach — realistic programmes, engineering sign-off before breaking ground, and site supervisors who stay until the punch list is empty. Today we run road, building, water and earthworks contracts concurrently across four regions, backed by our own plant fleet and a permanent core of engineers and site staff.

Our Mission

Deliver infrastructure that performs under Ghana's real conditions — heavy rains, high axle loads and long service life — on the timeline we quote.

Our Vision

Become the contractor Ghanaian municipalities and private developers call first, because our sites finish clean and on programme.

Our Values

Site safety, honest reporting to clients, and paying our crews and suppliers on time, every time.

Milestones

How We Got Here

2014

Company founded in Accra

Started with a single earthmoving crew serving private land-clearing contracts in the Greater Accra Region.

2017

First major road contract

Awarded a feeder-road grading and drainage contract in the Eastern Region, our first government tender.

2020

Building division launched

Expanded into commercial and institutional building construction, starting with a district market complex.

2023

Fleet and workshop expansion

Opened a dedicated plant workshop and grew the owned equipment fleet to reduce dependency on hired plant.

2026

Operating across four regions

Running concurrent road, water and building contracts in Greater Accra, Eastern, Ashanti and Central regions.
